About Savills GRDC

The first and leading branded residential advisors, Savills GRDC has been at the forefront of this sector since 2009. The team comprises two core divisions - Consultancy and Sales & Marketing - working in tandem to deliver a seamless end-to-end service from project conception to successful commercialisation.

Based in London and Dubai, our experts bring unrivalled experience across luxury large-scale mixed-use destination resorts and specialist standalone residential developments in urban and resort locations worldwide. Working closely with Savills’ international network of offices, we provide advisory grounded in a deep understanding of global property demand dynamics.

The Branded Residences Market

The branded residences sector has proven its resilience through periods of significant global uncertainty, growing over 180% in the past decade. The development pipeline continues to expand, with upcoming projects set to double current supply by the end of the forecast period.

In 2024, branded residences commanded a global average premium of 33% over non-branded equivalents, rising to 47% in emerging cities where luxury brands attract growing high-net-worth populations.

The sector is no longer the exclusive domain of luxury hotel brands. Non-hotel brands are entering in increasing numbers, catering to the evolving preferences of younger buyers who are reshaping amenity and service expectations globally, prioritising wellness facilities, flexible workspaces and larger living areas.

Dubai, South Florida, and New York remain the top destinations for branded residences, while emerging cities and resort locations are rapidly climbing the rankings.

Our Global Network

With over 170 years in residential property, Savills has built enduring relationships and a network of 700+ offices worldwide. Savills GRDC works closely with this network to ensure clients receive the most current market intelligence, buyer insight and transactional data available.

From market research and financial viability through to sales, marketing and capital markets, Savills advises across the full development lifecycle, de-risking and optimising projects from inception to completion.

What We Do

From initial concept through to sale, Savills GRDC provides end-to-end advisory across the full branded residential development lifecycle. Our five core service lines are delivered by specialist teams with deep market knowledge and a track record spanning 51 countries.

01
Feasibility Studies
Developers, investors & operators
We provide rigorous market analysis and financial feasibility assessments to establish whether a branded residential project is viable, well-positioned and commercially optimised before a developer commits capital. Our studies cover demand analysis, competitive benchmarking, pricing strategy, financial projections and brand fit assessment.
02
Brand Search
Developers
Identifying the right brand partner is one of the most critical decisions in a branded residences project. Savills GRDC manages the full brand search and selection process: from defining the brand brief and longlisting candidates to negotiating term sheets and finalising brand agreements on behalf of the developer.
03
Market Intelligence
Developers, investors & operators
Our in-house research team publishes annual global and regional reports on the branded residences sector, providing developers, investors and brand partners with authoritative data, pricing benchmarks and pipeline analysis. Bespoke market intelligence commissions are also available for specific geographies or asset types.
04
Operator Advisory
Developers & operators
Structuring branded residences, whether standalone or integrated developments, requires thoughtful consideration to ensure seamless operations and maximum synergistic impact. Savills GRDC offers guidance on rental programmes, HOA integration, residential agreements and budgeting, protecting stakeholders and delivering successful projects.
05
Brand Premium Studies
Operators
We quantify the price premium commanded by a specific brand in a specific market, providing developers with the evidence base to negotiate brand agreements and underwrite project finances. Our premium studies draw on Savills’ proprietary transaction data and comparable sales analysis across global branded residential markets.

Dragon Palace Estate
Development Consultancy Hotel Feasibility Financial Model Co-Branded
Location China
Brands LUX & Fendi
Period 2026
The Challenge
Dragon Palace Estate sits in a location with no history of high-value residential transactions: no pricing benchmark, no absorption data, and no precedent for branded residences at this scale in non-urban China. The client’s ambition was to price at global trophy-asset levels. Our research confirmed this as a credible end-state, but not a viable launch strategy.
The Outcome
A credible pricing framework projecting total GDV in excess of US$3 billion across a 7–8 year sell-out, combining market evidence, a four-phase pricing escalation strategy, full DCF model, and a stated opinion of value. A strategy that treats the project’s lack of comparables not as a weakness, but as the foundation of its pricing power.
01
Market Research & Benchmarking
  • No local pricing precedent: nearest comparables 130km away in Shanghai and Hangzhou
  • International UHNW buyer profiling and demand segmentation
  • Entry price point analysis: unit mix redesigned to bring threshold pricing to market-driven levels
02
Pricing & Sales Strategy
  • Four-phase pricing escalation strategy to establish velocity before margin
  • Each phase contingent on absorption targets being met
  • Market-making framework treating the absence of comparables as a pricing asset
03
Hotel Feasibility & Financial Modelling
  • Independent hotel feasibility with operator assumption stress-testing
