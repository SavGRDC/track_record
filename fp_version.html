About
Global leaders in branded residential advisory

Savills GRDC has been advising developers on branded residential projects since 2009. Our team specialises in development strategy, brand selection and the commercial positioning of luxury residential projects.

Based in London and Dubai, we advise on a wide spectrum of developments including large mixed use destination resorts and standalone residential projects in both urban and resort locations worldwide.

The Market
One of the fastest growing sectors in global real estate

The branded residences sector has expanded rapidly over the past decade, growing by more than 180% as developers increasingly partner with global brands to enhance product differentiation and pricing power.

In 2024, branded residences achieved an average global price premium of 33% over comparable non branded properties. In emerging luxury markets the premium rises to as much as 47% as international brands attract growing concentrations of high net worth buyers.

While the sector was historically dominated by luxury hotel operators, a new generation of non hotel brands is entering the market, broadening the branded residential landscape and responding to evolving buyer preferences.

Dubai, South Florida and New York remain the most established markets, while emerging cities and resort destinations continue to see strong development momentum.

Brand Search

Identifying the right brand partner

Savills GRDC manages the full brand search process, from defining the brand brief and identifying suitable partners to coordinating negotiations and supporting the execution of brand agreements.
