Savills GRDC

Services

Strategic advisory across the branded residential lifecycle. Savills GRDC supports developers and investors at every stage of a branded residential project, from early development strategy through to brand selection, operational structuring, capital formation and successful project sales.

Chapter One
Development Advisory

Development Strategy & Feasibility

Defining the optimal development strategy

Savills GRDC advises on the strategic foundations of a project, combining market demand analysis, competitive benchmarking, pricing strategy and financial modelling to determine the optimal positioning, scale and brand strategy for a development. Our feasibility studies assess market demand, pricing potential, development scale and financial viability, providing developers with the evidence required to proceed with confidence.

Brand Search

Identifying the right brand partner

Savills GRDC manages the full brand search process, from defining the brand brief and identifying suitable partners to coordinating negotiations and supporting the execution of brand agreements. Our global relationships with leading hotel and lifestyle brands ensure developers secure partners that enhance project positioning, pricing power and long term value.

Operator Advisory

Structuring projects for operational success

Savills GRDC advises on residential management structures, rental programmes, homeowners associations and operational budgeting to ensure projects operate smoothly and deliver the intended resident experience. This work is typically undertaken alongside brand negotiations and project design development.

Equity Raising

Securing capital to deliver the project

Savills GRDC advises developers on equity raising strategies, supporting the structuring and placement of capital alongside Savills' global capital markets network. Our team assists with investment positioning, investor outreach and transaction support, helping developers secure partners aligned with the long term vision of the project.

Chapter Two
Research & Intelligence

Market Intelligence

Industry-leading research on the branded residences sector

Savills GRDC produces industry leading research on the branded residences sector, including global and regional reports covering pricing benchmarks, development pipelines and market trends. Alongside these publications, the team delivers bespoke market intelligence assignments for developers, investors and operators seeking detailed insight into specific markets or project concepts.

Brand Premium Studies

Quantifying the value of brand affiliation

Savills GRDC quantifies the price premium associated with specific brands in specific markets, providing developers with the evidence required to negotiate brand agreements and underwrite project finances. These studies draw on proprietary transaction data and comparable analysis across global branded residential markets.

Chapter Three
Sales & Commercialisation

Residential Sales

Delivering successful project commercialisation

Through Savills' global residential sales platform, developers gain access to one of the most established international buyer networks in luxury real estate. Working alongside Savills' specialist sales teams, Savills GRDC supports the development of sales strategies, pricing structures and marketing programmes that maximise absorption and pricing performance. This integrated approach allows projects to move seamlessly from development strategy through to successful sales execution.
